# Requirements Management as a Foundational Discipline

Requirements management — the elicitation, writing, and verification of clear, understandable requirements across the whole lifecycle — is a core discipline in our organisation and the basis for all subsequent operations.

## Description

Requirements management underpins all subsequent engineering operations: without elicited, written, and verified requirements, development and testing lack a stable and correct basis. This goal commits the organisation to maintain requirements management as an excelled, continuously practised discipline across the whole product lifecycle.
