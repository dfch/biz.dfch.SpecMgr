    What are the ISO 25010 quality characteristics?

 2.


    *ISO/IEC 25010* is an international standard that defines a quality
    model for evaluating software products and systems. It identifies
    quality characteristics that help assess how well a software product
    meets user and stakeholder requirements.

    The standard has *two main quality models*:


        1. Product Quality Characteristics

    The product quality model defines *9 quality characteristics* in the
    2023 edition (ISO/IEC 25010:2023).

    #	Quality Characteristic	Description
    1	*Functional Suitability*	The degree to which the software provides
    functions that meet specified requirements and user needs.
    2	*Performance Efficiency*	How efficiently the software performs in
    terms of response time, resource usage and capacity.
    3	*Compatibility*	The ability of the software to exchange
    information and operate alongside other systems.
    4	*Interaction Capability*	How easily users can interact with the
    software to achieve their goals.
    5	*Reliability*	The ability of the software to perform consistently
    under specified conditions.
    6	*Security*	The ability to protect information and data against
    unauthorized access and threats.
    7	*Maintainability*	How easily the software can be modified,
    corrected, tested and improved.
    8	*Flexibility*	The ability of the software to adapt to changes in
    environments, requirements or usage.
    9	*Safety*	The ability of the software to avoid unacceptable risks
    of harm to people, property or the environment.


        2. Quality-in-Use Characteristics

    The quality-in-use model evaluates the quality of a software product
    from the user's perspective.

    It includes 5 characteristics:

    #	Quality Characteristic	Description
    1	*Effectiveness*	The accuracy and completeness with which users
    achieve their goals.
    2	*Efficiency*	The resources and effort required for users to
    achieve their goals.
    3	*Satisfaction*	The degree to which users are satisfied with their
    experience using the software.
    4	*Freedom from Risk*	The extent to which using the software avoids
    potential risks to users, organizations and the environment.
    5	*Context Coverage*	The ability of the software to be used
    effectively in different contexts and environments.


        Important: ISO 25010:2011 vs. ISO 25010:2023

    If you are studying software quality or preparing an academic
    project, it is important to know that there are two versions:

      * *ISO/IEC 25010:2011:* Defines 8 product quality characteristics
        and 5 quality-in-use characteristics.
      * *ISO/IEC 25010:2023:* Updates the product quality model to 9
        characteristics, with changes to the terminology and structure.


          Quick Summary

    Model	ISO 25010:2011	ISO 25010:2023
    Product Quality	8 characteristics	9 characteristics
    Quality-in-Use	5 characteristics	5 characteristics

    *Recommendation:* If you are developing a software evaluation
    questionnaire, thesis, or research project, make sure you specify
    which ISO/IEC 25010 version you are following, because the
    characteristics and their subcharacteristics differ.
