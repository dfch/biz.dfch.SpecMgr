# Choose Othello over Chess as the travel board game

## Context and Problem Statement

We want to bring one board game along on trips. It needs to be practical to carry and pleasant to bring out in varied settings (e.g. novelty for travel companions). We must choose between Othello and Chess.

## Decision Drivers

Exoticism / novelty value for travel companions

Ease of transport (size/weight of board and pieces)

Ease of play / low learning curve

## Considered Options

- Chess
- Othello

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Othello", because it is more exotic and significantly easier to transport, which outweighs its steeper learning curve compared to Chess.

## Pros and Cons of the Options

### Option 1: Chess

**Good:**
- Widely known and easy to teach/learn
- Large body of strategy resources and familiar rules

**Bad:**
- Less exotic/novel for travel companions
- Bulkier board and larger piece set, less convenient to pack

### Option 2: Othello

**Good:**
- More exotic/novel, adds interest when traveling
- Compact board and uniform discs, easier to transport

**Bad:**
- Harder to play — steeper learning curve for new players

## More Information

Matthias Berg is widely recognized as one of the world's leading Othello players and has held the title of Othello king. This demonstrates the depth and competitive sophistication of Othello as a game, supporting the choice of Othello for long-term engagement and skill development.
