# Level 1 Heading is the Title of the Problem Statement

## Current State

### Summary

Free-form synthesis of the current state, drawn from whichever of the 7
5W2H questions below are actually answered. Mandatory, but a single
sentence like this one is sufficient even if every question below is
still unanswered.

### What Is the Problem?

A short blind-text answer describing what the problem actually is. Optional.

### Why Is It a Problem?

A short blind-text answer describing why this matters. Optional.

### Where Is the Problem Observed?

A short blind-text answer describing where the problem is observed. Optional.

### Who Is Impacted?

A short blind-text answer describing who is impacted. Optional.

### When Was the Problem First Observed?

A short blind-text answer describing when the problem was first observed. Optional.

### How Is the Problem Observed?

A short blind-text answer describing how the problem is observed/detected. Optional.

### How Often Is the Problem Observed?

A short blind-text answer describing how often the problem recurs. Optional.

## Gap

A short blind-text statement of the measurable, actual-vs-expected
difference between the current and future state. Mandatory -- kept a pure
measurement, not conflated with `Impact`.

## Impact

A short blind-text statement of the business/cost/safety consequence of
the gap. Optional.

## Future State

A short blind-text statement of the desired/target condition once the
problem is resolved. Mandatory.

## References

- A short blind-text bullet list of free-form cross-references to other
  artifacts/tickets. Optional.

## More Information

This optional section can contain additional information. There is no defined format for this section.
